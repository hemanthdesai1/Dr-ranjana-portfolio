Research Focus & Vision

Molecular Probes & Bioimaging

Synthesis of nitronaphthalimide-based optical & electrochemical sensors for the selective detection of gasotransmitters (H₂S) and fungal pathogen imaging.

Flexible & Wearable Sensors

Engineering functional molecule–nanocarbon composite interfaces to boost electron-transfer kinetics for rapid, wearable diagnostic platforms.

Point-of-Care Diagnostics

Translating fundamental chemistry into accessible, low-cost analytical devices for clinical monitoring, environmental surveillance, and safety.
